Volume Box: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Volume Box ("the app"), by Sleepy Owl Apps, is a free volume booster, equalizer and music player for Android. There is no sign-up and no account. This policy explains what data is involved when you use it.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Settings and lists: your boost, equalizer, presets, playlists, favourite stations, podcast subscriptions, recent links and the dates you last unlocked online mode and boost above 200%. These are stored only on your device and deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Your music library: with your permission, the app reads the list of audio files on your phone to show and play them. The list never leaves your device.
  • Music server sign-in (optional): if you connect your own music server, the address and username are stored on your phone and the password is stored in Android's encrypted storage. They are sent only to the server you entered.

Notification access (optional)

If you turn on "Notification access" for the Other apps panel, the app uses it only to see what other music apps are playing and to control them (play, pause, skip). It does not read, store or share the content of your notifications.

Online services the app contacts

When you use online features, your device connects directly to:

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info) for the station directory, and the radio stations you play
  • Apple Podcasts search and charts (itunes.apple.com) to find podcasts, and the podcast hosts whose episodes you play
  • Links you paste and your own music server, if you use them

These services receive normal connection information (such as your IP address) as part of any internet request. Their own privacy policies apply.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob to stay free. Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads, and in some regions to personalise them.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads & privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-up, or personal profile.
  • We don't sell your data, and the developer does not run any server that receives your data.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.
